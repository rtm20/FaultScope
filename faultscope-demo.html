Recording format & instrument connection

Use the downloadable sample structure: asset_id, units and 2-10,000 samples. Every sample has time_s, pressure_bar, flow_l_min and temperature_c. Units: s, bar, L/min, C. Missing readings may be null and are flagged.

Input path: sensors → existing recorder → exported file. Vendor formats and direct instrument connections need separately validated adapters. No PLC, MQTT or OPC UA connection exists in this demo.
